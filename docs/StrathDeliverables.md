Strathclyde requirements

## 1. Statement of Purpose, within two weeks of arrival at the host institution:

This should detail the deliverables and schedule for the project. This will be agreed with the supervisor, who will take on the role of client. The statement of purpose will form a contract between the group and the client. The front page should list the group members, the supervisor's name and email address and the project title. This should be emailed to Dr McLaren as a single PDF. Please explain to your supervisor that they will be asked to complete a marking form at the end of the project, which will contribute to your final assessment.

## 2. Interim Report, submission date Monday 2nd November 2026, 10pm (UK time) (10 marks):

This should describe the progress so far, and describe your plan for the remainder of the project. You should also reflect on how you are working as a group, and managing your time and the project. This should be 10-15 pages in length (maximum). The report should be uploaded as a single PDF to the link below. (Please note that there are NO extended guidelines for the report. At this stage in your careers, you should be able to decide what format and content for the interim report should be, based on the parameters described in the brief guidance, above. This is a little different from the 4th year, where you are led through each stage, but this is a more realistic scenario for graduate level engineers.) You will be given feedback on your report.

## 3. Final report, showcase video and presentation, including supervisor mark, second semester, Monday 11th January 2027, 12 noon (UK time) (90 marks)

The group should produce a final technical report in a format to be agreed with the supervisor at the host institution (note that the content and format should be agreed with your supervisor, no detailed guidance and rules will be issued by us). The report should include an executive summary (see below). One copy of the combined report should be uploaded as a single PDF document to the ME535 MyPlace page, using the upload tool (below) by 12 noon on Mon 11th January 2027. In addition, each group should create a short "Showcase Video" (details below), which should be uploaded by the same deadline. Note that extension requests are not possible.

On return, each group will be assessed on the extent to which they have met the deliverables set out in the contract, as well as on the quality of reflection on the group process and project management experience. This will be assessed by a one hour group presentation/discussion/oral exam with Strathclyde staff during the semester 2 development week (11th to 15th January 2027). Full details of the assessment arrangements and the marking scheme will be provided well in advance. The final assessment contributes 90% of the project mark and includes a contribution from your supervisor.
